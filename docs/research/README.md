# Research: Tuition Class Manager in Sri Lanka

Research done in November 2026 to answer one question: **is this idea worth building for Sri Lanka, and if so, what shape should it take?**

## The short version

- **The market is real and very large.** Most O/L and A/L students go to tuition. Around 6 in 10 households pay for it, and private tutoring is the biggest single part of what families spend on education.
- **The idea is not new.** At least eight Sri Lankan products already do QR attendance and send parents a "your child arrived" SMS. One claims more than 1,200 institutes as clients. **The arrival SMS can't be our main selling point on its own.**
- **Prices are already low.** Competitors charge from free up to about Rs. 2,000 a month for a teacher with 200 students. SMS costs about Rs. 0.50–0.85 each, which is a real share of that price.
- **There is still room**, but only if we win on something specific. The strongest openings we found:
  1. Collecting fees through **LankaQR**. Since April 2026 there is no merchant fee on payments up to Rs. 5,000, and most monthly class fees are below that.
  2. Telling parents when their child **did not** arrive. This is more valuable than telling them when the child did.
  3. Being built **Sinhala and Tamil first**, and working **offline** on a cheap Android phone in a crowded hall.
  4. Serving **tuition institutes** that host many teachers. They need to split fees between teachers and the institute, and few products handle that well.
- **The law matters.** Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act has been fully in force since March 2025. We will hold children's data, so privacy has to be designed in from day one.

**Recommendation:** go ahead, but treat the next step as validation, not building. Talk to 15–20 teachers and 3–5 institutes before locking the tech stack or the pricing. Details are in [04](04-analysis-and-recommendations.md).

## Notes on accuracy

- Figures come from public sources: research papers, news articles and competitor websites. Competitor prices were checked in November 2026 and may change.
- Some sources disagree. Estimates of the industry's size range from Rs. 16 billion to over Rs. 200 billion a year. Where sources conflict, we show the range rather than pick one number.
- Nothing here is legal advice. Before launch, have a lawyer confirm the data protection and SMS rules.
